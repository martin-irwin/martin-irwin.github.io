Before you plug it in pp. 1, 6, 32, 108, 113

AC 100 V, 50/60 Hz only. The manual states this is a Japan-only model and must not be run on any other voltage (fire and shock risk).

DVD: region 2 or ALL, NTSC only. Video CDs must also be NTSC. A disc in another TV system gives a broken picture.

Not in the manual, for use in Europe: on 230 V mains the unit needs a step-down transformer to 100 V; rated consumption is 70 W, so allow comfortable headroom above that. European DVDs are region 2 but PAL, so they fall outside what the manual supports. The FM tuner covers 76–90 MHz only, so of the European band (87.5–108 MHz) just the lowest 2.5 MHz is reachable.

  • The disc slot is a slot loader. Insert discs at the centre of the slot with the label side to the right. 8 cm CDs must be centred or they will not be pulled in.
  • Do not load transparent discs (the loader detects discs optically), shaped discs, cracked or warped discs, or discs with stuck-on labels or stabiliser rings. Printable CD-R/DVD-R label surfaces can stick and jam.
  • Discs and MDs cannot be inserted or ejected in standby. Do not force them.
  • Memory: the clock is lost the moment the plug is pulled. Presets, volume, tone, timer and display settings survive about one day unplugged.
  • Condensation after a move from cold to warm stops the unit working. Leave it a few hours.
  • Before transporting: remove the disc and MD, press MD ▶/II, then DVD/CD ▶/II, wait for the display to change, wait a few seconds more, then power off.
  • Never use lens cleaner discs or contact cleaner spray.

Microcomputer reset

If keys stop responding, the display shows nonsense, or a disc will not eject (LOCKED): unplug the mains lead, hold the power key on the unit, and plug it back in while holding. This returns everything to factory settings, including the parental password.

Basic operation pp. 21–26

  • Power: the power key, or press any source key in standby (DVD/CD ▶/II, MD ▶/II, TUNER, AUX) and the unit switches on and starts that source.
  • Demo display: appears after about 5 seconds idle with disc and MD stopped. Press demo during the demo to turn it off. It comes back after any power cut.
  • Volume: 0–39, then MAX. MUTE toggles.
  • SOUND (remote): cycles EX.BASS (bass boost at any volume) → LOUDNESS (bass and treble lift for low volume) → off.
  • TONE (remote): TONE, adjust BASS with ⏮ ⏭, TONE again for TREBLE, TONE to finish. Range −8 to +8. Disabled while V.F.S. is on.
  • BYPASS (remote): sends the source past the SOUND, TONE and V.F.S. circuits.
  • External source: AUX, then start the other device. Match its level under AUX INPUT (−3 to +3); this also changes the MD recording level.

Setting the clock (unit only)

  1. mode → TIME ADJUST → set
  2. 12 HOUR or 24 HOUR → set
  3. Day of week → set
  4. Hour → set, minute → set

Timers do not work until the clock is set. In standby, STOP ■ shows day and time for 5 seconds. Redo the clock after any power interruption.

Radio pp. 27–30

  • Tune: select the band with TUNER FM/AM (remote TUNER/BAND). tuning mode on the unit or AUTO/MONO on the remote switches between auto tuning (AUTO lit, stereo, stops at each station) and manual (step by step, FM in mono). Then ⏮ ⏭ on the unit or ◀◀ ▶▶ on the remote. On the unit, press ⏮ ⏭ while AUTO or MANUAL is still showing.
  • Store a preset (remote): tune the station, ENTER, number 1–40, ENTER.
  • Recall: ⏮ ⏭ or the number keys.
  • Delete (remote): recall the preset, CLEAR/DEL., ENTER. Later presets move up one. Preset 40 cannot be deleted.
  • DISPLAY cycles station name, frequency, clock.

Japan only: the auto preset (mode → ケンメイセッテイ → pick a prefecture → set) fills up to 40 presets and labels FM stations from a built-in list of Japanese broadcasters. It overwrites existing presets. Outside Japan use manual presets.

Play modes (remote)

  • Programme (CD, Video CD): STOP ■ → P.MODE until PGM lights → track number → SET → repeat for up to 32 tracks → DVD/CD ▶/II. While stopped, CLEAR/DEL. removes the last entry and P.MODE clears everything. Eject or power-off also clears it.
  • Random: RANDOM with PGM off. ⏭ jumps to another track. Stops after one pass.
  • Repeat: REPEAT cycles one (REPEAT ONE; chapter on DVD) → all (title on DVD; folder, then whole disc, on MP3/JPEG) → off. Pressed during random play it repeats the random pass.
  • A–B repeat: A↔B at the start point, again at the end point, again to cancel. Not available in programme or random play.

Menus

  • DVD: TOP MENU or MENU, then cursor keys and ENTER, or a number key.
  • Video CD with playback control: menus run automatically. To use programme, random, repeat or recording, turn it off first: STOP ■ then P.B.C. (toggles). In menus ENTER selects, RETURN goes up a level, ⏮ ⏭ change page.
  • JPEG: JPEG LIST shows thumbnails; pick with the cursor and ENTER. Slides advance every 10 seconds by default; during the show ◀◀ ▶▶ step the interval through roughly 5, 2, 1, 0.5 and 0.2 seconds (scale below), and cursor ◀ ▶ rotates by 90°. With MP3/WMA on the same disc the picture stays up while the music plays.

Picture adjustment

Separate settings for Interlace and Progressive. Use the TV's own controls first if it has them.

  • Presets: Normal, Fine (sharper edges), Soft (less noise), Cinema.
  • User Mode 1 and 2: Contrast, Color, Sharpness (−7 to +7), Brightness (0 to +15). Progressive adds I/P conversion: AUTO (default), Film, Video.
  • Noise Reduction: Mosquito NR (On 2, On 1, Off), Block NR, 3D NR, Dot NR. 3D and Dot cannot both be on. Shared between interlace and progressive, as is GAMMA.
  • If a progressive picture looks wrong on a particular disc, switch to interlace with PROGRESSIVE/INTERLACE.

MD playback pp. 56–64

  • Insert the MD in the direction of its arrow, shutter edge up. MD ▶/II plays and pauses, STOP ■ stops, ⏮ ⏭ skip, hold ◀◀ ▶▶ to search, number keys select (+100 available), MD ⏏ on the unit ejects.
  • MDLP is supported. LP2, LP4 or MONO shows the mode of the track playing; nothing lit means standard stereo.
  • TIME cycles: track elapsed, track remaining, disc or group elapsed, total remaining, and recordable time left (marked R).
  • An untitled disc shows dots; an empty one shows BLANK DISC.

Group play (remote, stopped)

P.MODE until GROUP lights → GROUP SEARCH → cursor ▲ ▼ to pick the group → ENTER → MD ▶/II. To leave, press P.MODE twice while stopped.

Programme, random, repeat

Same keys as for CD: programme up to 32 tracks, RANDOM, REPEAT (one → all → off). In group mode, random and repeat stay inside the group. Unlike CD, STOP ■ does not cancel repeat on MD.

  • Set the mode and other options in the mode menu first (REC MODE, REC INPUT, TRACK MARK, REC LEVEL, TEXT COPY, GROUP MAKE).
  • Only audio CD records digitally. DVD, Video CD, MP3 and WMA always record in analogue at normal speed.

  • Auto track marks: from the CD's own track data in digital; at track changes in analogue; after about 2 seconds of silence from AUX; every 5 or 10 minutes from the radio. In MANUAL, press MD EDIT during recording to drop a mark.

  • Never cut the power while WRITING is showing. The recording or edit is lost.
  • The write-protect tab on the MD must be closed to record.

Basic recording (unit)

  1. Insert a recordable MD.
  2. Ready the source: load a disc and pause it with DVD/CD ▶/II, or tune a station, or press AUX.
  3. rec: REC blinks (record-pause).
  4. rec again, then start the source.

MD ▶/II pauses and resumes. STOP ■ stops both MD and disc.

Synchro recording from a point on a CD

Pause the CD where you want to start → rec → DVD/CD ▶/II. Both start together and stop at the end of the CD.

O.T.E. one-touch recording

  1. Load the MD and the disc. Press DVD/CD ▶/II then STOP ■.
  2. mode → O.T.E. SPEED → set → HIGH (4×, audio CD with digital input only) or NORMAL → set → MELODY ON or OFF (chime when finished) → set.
  3. mode → O.T.E. START → set.
  • Whole disc: start from stop. One track: play that track first, then do step 3; it rewinds to the track start and records it. A programme: build the programme, leave it stopped, then step 3.
  • Shortcut: once the speed is set, the remote's O.T.E. key does step 3 in one press.
  • MP3/WMA folder: with the disc stopped, DIRECTORY, choose the folder, then O.T.E.. Only files directly in that folder are recorded; JPEGs are skipped.
  • At 4× the speakers are silent. A track recorded at 4× cannot be recorded at 4× again for 74 minutes (Wait with a countdown); record at normal speed instead.
  • DVD audio can only be recorded one track at a time, and some discs refuse.

MD editing pp. 76–88

Remote only, source set to MD. Leave group, programme and random modes first. The pattern is always:

MD EDIT → ⏮ ⏭ to choose TRACK, GROUP or CANCEL → SET → choose the operation → SET → choose tracks, SET after each → ENTER to execute.

The display shows EDIT NOW then COMPLETE, or CAN'T EDIT. Edits are written to the disc when you eject it. Twenty seconds without a key press abandons the edit; MD EDIT also backs out.

Undo

MD EDIT → CANCEL → SET → ENTER restores the disc to how it was when inserted. This is no longer possible once the disc has been ejected or anything new has been recorded.

Erasing every track in a group removes the group. A moved or divided track takes the group of its neighbours. Groups made here can display as stray digits in the disc title on players without group support.

MD titles pp. 89–92

  1. TITLE INPUT (not in programme or random mode).
  2. ⏮ ⏭ to choose DISC or a track (T001…) → SET.
  3. Enter the text, then ENTER to store it. Repeat from step 2 for more.
  4. TITLE INPUT to finish, then eject to write the disc.

For a group title, enter group play mode and select the group first; the list then starts with G01.

  • CHARAC. cycles the character set: letters and symbols, digits and symbols, half-width katakana.
  • Number keys are multi-tap: 2 gives A, B, C, a, b, c. +10 and +100 hold the symbols. SET confirms each character.
  • ◀◀ ▶▶ move the cursor, CLEAR/DEL. deletes, SPACE inserts a space.
  • Limits: 80 characters per track, 1792 per disc (fewer with katakana).
  • Avoid /, - and ; in disc titles on grouped discs; group data is stored in the same field.

Timers pp. 93–98

Sleep (remote): SLEEP steps 10, 20 … 90 minutes, then off. One press later shows the time left.

PROG.1 and PROG.2 (unit)

Set the clock first. Load the disc or MD the timer will use.

  1. mode → TIMER SET → set → PROG.1 or PROG.2 → set → ON → set.
  2. Days: Everyday, a single weekday (then ONETIME or EVERY WEEK), Mon-Friday, Tue-Saturday, or Sat,Sunday.
  3. On time, then off time (hour, then minute).
  4. Type: PLAY, AI PLAY (volume fades up to the set level) or REC.
  5. Volume. For an unattended recording set it to 0.
  6. Source: TUNER (then the preset number), DVD/CD, MD, AUX. For REC only tuner or AUX, followed by the recording mode.
  7. Switch to standby. The standby/timer lamp turns green.
  • Keep the two programmes at least a minute apart. Start a radio recording about a minute early to avoid clipping the opening.
  • Remote TIMER cycles which programmes are armed: 1, 2, both, none. Settings are kept when disarmed.
  • Lamp blinking green means the power was interrupted: reset the clock and re-arm.

Common faults

  • standby/timer lamp blinks red, no sound: the speaker leads are shorted. Power off and reconnect. If it persists, stop using the unit and have it serviced.
  • Lamp blinks green, or the clock is frozen and blinking: power was interrupted. Set the clock again.
  • No sound: check volume, MUTE, and whether headphones are plugged in.
  • No picture: TV input, cables, and that the disc's TV system matches the TV.
  • Prohibition icon on screen: the disc does not allow that operation at that point.
  • Forgot the parental code: do the microcomputer reset.
  • AUX recording has wrong track numbers: adjust AUX INPUT, or set TRACK MARK to MANUAL.
  • MD quirks that are normal: free time does not grow after erasing short tracks; elapsed plus remaining does not add up exactly; brief dropouts when searching across edited tracks; some edited tracks cannot be combined.
